## Shulker Preview {#shulker}

Hold [[Left Shift]] over a shulker box to read its 27 slots and amounts in the tooltip. The background follows the tint of the box. The key accepts keyboard keys and mouse buttons, and there are options to always show the view or to disable the tint.

:::figure shulker
Press and hold the box.
:::

## Item information {#items}

- **Item Details** adds the exact remaining durability, the item identifier and the stack limit, each with its own option.
- **Inventory Alert** warns when the main inventory is full or has few free slots, with a configurable threshold and time between warnings.
- **Durability Warning** gives a heads up before your armor or a tool breaks.

## Movement and camera {#camera}

- **Zoom** while you hold [[C]].
- **Freelook** while you hold [[Left Alt]].
- **Toggle Sprint**, **Smooth Camera**, **Smooth Hotbar**, **Camera Tweaks** and **Auto F5**, which switches to third person while a container is open.

## Checkpoints and radar {#checkpoints}

**Checkpoints** saves places, drawn in the world with beams and labels, with colors, names and visibility. The saved names default to "Checkpoint 3" and so on, and names you typed are never changed. Death Point stores where you died and shows distance and an arrow relative to where you look.

**Radar** is a compass ring that shows your checkpoints of the current world, the players the server already sends to the vanilla locator bar and the players you have in sight. On servers it never shows anyone through walls: line of sight is checked ten times per second.

## Macros {#macros}

Macros run commands on a key or on a game event: pressing a key, killing a player, dying, joining a server or low health.

## Performance {#performance}

- **Performance Monitor** is a frame-time graph with FPS computed from it, the 1% lows, P95 and memory use. The history keeps 1024 samples.
- **Performance** includes *Unfocused FPS*, 30 by default, so the game does not run at full speed in the background.
- **FPS Boost** turns heavy video settings down and restores them when you switch it off.
- **Larp FPS** changes only the FPS number shown by the FPS widget and the Info Bar, within a range you choose, either random or fixed. Real FPS and the frame limit are not changed.

## Profiles and share codes {#profiles}

Profiles has six named slots, with date, an *In use* tag and the count of unsaved changes. You can save, load, rename and delete them.

A **share code** (`SKELL1-…`) copies your whole configuration, with modules, settings and HUD positions, as text that you paste on another computer. Importing keeps your keys and the texts you wrote.
