Interface & controls

Open the menu, find modules, switch language and pick a settings layout.

Opening and moving the menu

Press Right Shift in a world or on the title screen. The key can be changed in Options, Controls, Skell Client. The window opens centered and can be dragged from its top bar, the area of the logo and the title. It may leave the screen partly, but the bar always stays reachable: fully visible vertically and at least 120 px horizontally, also after a resolution or GUI scale change. Sliders, buttons and text fields never move the window.

The sidebar

The sidebar groups the pages in four blocks. The counter of each module page shows how many modules are on out of the total.

BlockPages
ModulesHUD, PvP, Utility, Visuals
StyleCosmetics, Kill Effects
ToolsCheckpoints, Macros, Discord
ClientProfiles, Client

On narrow windows the sidebar keeps only the icons, with a tooltip, and on short windows it becomes more compact. At the bottom you find your profile and shortcuts to Edit HUD, Recordings and the Skell folder.

About the demo on this site

The interactive demo recreates the module pages and the Client page with the real catalog. The Kill Effects, Checkpoints, Macros, Discord and Profiles pages exist in the client but are not recreated on the web.

Finding a module

Each module page has chips for its subgroups, with a counter:

  • HUD: Info, Player, Media, Game UI.
  • PvP: Combat info, Stats.
  • Utility: Movement, Capture, Inventory, World, System.
  • Visuals: Combat, World, Camera, Hands & motion, Player, Effects.

The Enabled chip leaves only what is on. Anything marked NEW was added in the current version, and new items are listed before the rest of their group, after your favorites. Mark a card as favorite to keep it first.

To search, press Ctrl F or start typing. Enter opens the first result, and the results keep their category.

Settings of a module

The settings button of a card opens its settings with a live preview on top: the real HUD widget, the cosmetic in 3D or the kill effect animation. Below it you get Enabled, Keybind and the position in the HUD, then every setting in groups with a short description. Reset to defaults is at the bottom.

The controls are the same everywhere: switches, sliders with their value, a segmented selector for up to three options or a dropdown for more, a color picker with saturation, hue, alpha, quick colors and hex, and a text field with cursor, paste and select-all.

Two layouts

Client, Menus, Settings layout chooses where settings appear. Both draw the same content, so switching never resets anything.

  • Sheet grows out of the module card and covers the window.
  • Side panel shows wide cards in two columns and keeps the settings beside the window. A click on the card opens its settings. The switch, or a right click, turns the module on.

When the window is too narrow, the client uses the sheet automatically.

Client settings

The Client page controls the accent color, the settings layout, animations and the language.

  • Accent color tints switches, selections, borders and focus across the menu. It supports a custom color.
  • Animations can be Full, Reduced or Off. Reduced shortens movement and Off removes it, including the fade between menus and the intro.
  • Language is English or Spanish. Anything without a translation appears in English, and notifications follow the language of the client.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Right ShiftOpen or close the menu
Ctrl F or typingSearch modules
EnterOpen the first result
Tab / Shift TabNext or previous page
EscClose in order: dropdown, settings, search, menu
CZoom, while held
Left AltFreelook, while held
GInspect a sword model
BEmote wheel
F8Save the last 30 seconds with Instant Replay
Left ShiftShulker Preview, while held over a shulker box

Every key can be changed in Options, Controls, Skell Client. Open HUD Editor, Screenshot gallery, the stopwatch and the music keys have no default key.

Two keys per module

Zoom, Freelook, Sword Models and Instant Replay show two rows in their settings: the key you hold or press to use the feature, and the key that only switches the module on or off.
